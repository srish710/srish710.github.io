A LITTLE ABOUT ME

I’m Srishti, an Electronic Systems Engineering Technology student at Texas A&M. I want to design interfaces for physical products, and build the hardware underneath them too.

Engineering’s given me an eye for constraints: battery life, sensor limits, what a microcontroller can actually handle in real time. Product design is where those constraints are useful; they tell me exactly what a person needs to use and understand, and what not.
